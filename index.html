/ 02 — About

A small shop
with a big
collection.

We started as a couple of friends with too many clothes and not enough closet space. Now we run two shops — Arizona and right here in Provo — sourcing the good stuff so you don't have to dig through it yourself.

Every piece in the store is hand-picked. We're not into mass resale or whatever's trending this week. We're into clothes that were made to last and have already proven they can.
